# /no-ai-slop - Remove AI Slop & Edit Drafts

$ARGUMENTS

---

## Purpose

This command activates the `no-ai-slop` skill. It edits writing into sharper, more human prose while preserving personal voice and style, or detects AI-slop patterns without rewriting.

---

## Modes

1. **Edit (Default)**: Provide a draft to remove AI slop patterns (binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, faux insights, buzzwords, filler phrases) while preserving vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, and personal voice.
2. **Detect (`/no-ai-slop is this slop? ...`)**: Identify and quote every slop pattern found along with quick suggested fixes, without guessing whether AI wrote it.
