# KudosWall Blog & Content Strategy

## 🎯 Goals

Turn practical search demand into product-qualified interest by teaching small businesses how to:

1. Collect written, photo, and video testimonials.
2. Review and approve submissions.
3. Publish and embed them seamlessly on their own websites.

**Core Focus:** Prioritize content that naturally leads to a shareable collection link or an embeddable testimonial wall widget.

---

## 🔑 Topics & Keywords to Prioritize

### Primary Pillars

- **Collection Workflows:** How to collect customer testimonials (email, post-project, and video-request workflows).
- **Video Review Best Practices:** Video review capture, high-converting testimonial questions, consent/release protocols, approval pipelines, and repurposing.
- **Platform-Specific Embeds:** Accurate, step-by-step implementation guides for embedding testimonials on platforms like Carrd, Teachable, Framer, Webflow, Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace, and Notion (only when we can provide exact, tested implementation steps).
- **Tool Selection & Alternatives:** Honest comparisons and guides for free testimonial software, testimonial management software, Wall of Love tools, and transparent alternative analyses.
- **Small Business Use Cases:** Practical workflows tailored for coaches, marketing agencies, freelancers, SaaS companies, course creators, and service businesses.

### 🚫 What to Avoid

- Thin "best tools" roundup lists.
- Unsupported or exaggerated performance claims.
- Generic, robotic AI-generated marketing fluff.
- Fake review guidance or unethical social proof tactics.
- Pages targeting every platform or industry without a distinct workflow.
- Competitor comparisons that rely on unverified feature or pricing claims.

---

## 💡 Differentiation & Editorial Voice

### Competitive Positioning

- **Competitors** (Senja, Testimonial.to, Endorsal, Trustmary, Vocal Video, Boast, Famewall, VideoAsk) often focus on broad directories, heavy integrations, or complex feedback/survey positioning.
- **Our Edge:** We win through **clean, frictionless small-business workflows**:
  1. Collect written, photo, or video feedback via **one clean link**.
  2. Review and approve submissions with granular control.
  3. Embed approved social proof with lightweight, high-performance widgets.

### Editorial Guidelines

- **Tone:** Direct, helpful, honest, and practical.
- **Structure:** Short paragraphs, descriptive H2 headers, actionable checklists, concrete request copy examples, and clear step-by-step implementation sequences.
- **Screenshots & Visuals:** Show screenshots or step sequences where available.
- **Transparency:** State limitations plainly and credit third-party tools/vendors accurately.
- **Problem-First:** Open with the reader's immediate problem, solve it thoroughly before introducing any product CTA.
- **Search-Intent FAQs:** Include FAQs only when they address genuine search demand and user queries.

---

## 🏷️ Brand Insertion Rules

1. **Brandless Openings:** Open evergreen guides completely brandless. Deliver genuine, standalone value before mentioning KudosWall.
2. **First Brand Mention:** The first mention should be a contextual "Try it" CTA placed _after_ the foundational educational sections.
   - **Deep Linking:** Always deep-link to the most specific relevant page/feature on our site (e.g., `/embed-testimonials`, `/free`, `/testimonial-questions`), **never** the generic bare homepage.
3. **Mid-Article Utility Tie-Ins:**
   - Mention KudosWall only as a direct utility tie-in naming the exact feature that solves the step just described in a single sentence, then immediately move back to educational content.
   - **Limit:** Maximum 1 brand touch per section; **never** in two consecutive sections.
4. **"How to do this with KudosWall" Walkthrough:**
   - Near the end of the article, provide a dedicated, concrete walkthrough showing exact steps and honest limitations.
   - **Superlative Limit:** Exactly **one** superlative positioning sentence allowed per article, and _only_ within this section.
5. **Third-Party Attribution:** Credit third-party tools/vendors plainly and objectively.
6. **FAQ Rules:** Keep FAQs strictly subject-focused, allowing brand mentions only for factual availability or compatibility answers.
